Step 1 of 5

Find World of Warcraft

The companion reads the RestedRealm addon's save in your World of Warcraft: Forever folder.

Step 2 of 5

Install the addon

The addon notes what you see while you play: quests, characters, merchants and more. It shows nothing on screen and never slows the game.

World of Warcraft is open. After installing, type /reload in game or restart it.

Step 3 of 5

Connect your account

Connect this PC to your RestedRealm account, so your uploads count for you. It takes one click in your browser.

Choose Connect. Your browser opens restedrealm.com, where you sign in with Discord if needed and confirm. This window updates by itself.

Finish in your browser. When you confirm there, allow it to open RestedRealm Companion.

Use a code instead

On restedrealm.com, open , choose Create a code and enter it here within five minutes.

This PC is connected to your RestedRealm account.

Step 4 of 5

What gets sent

Sent to RestedRealm

  • What you meet in the world: quests, creatures, items, merchants and trainers, with their IDs, names, rewards and prices
  • Where and when: the zone, map position and time of each record
  • Your character's realm, race, class, faction and level, never its name
  • The game version and language, and private IDs so repeated uploads count once

Stays on this PC

  • Full quest text and character dialogue
  • Your character's name and other players
  • Your Battle.net and Discord login details
  • Anything from other addons, chat or the game's memory

Upload automatically

After you log out, quit or type /reload. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Start with Windows

Step 5 of 5

You're all set

Play as usual. Your data uploads after you log out, quit or type /reload, because that is when the game saves it.

Closing this window keeps the companion running next to the Windows clock. Click its icon there to open it again.
